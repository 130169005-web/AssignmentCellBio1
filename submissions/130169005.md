The Paradox of Cell Death.
Cell division is crucial for complex life.
The ability to undergo programmed cell death is also vital.
Apoptosis is a highly controlled and elegant method of cell death.
In contrast to necrosis,where there will be swelling in the cell and pain and inflammations, apoptosis is a very clean process.
When the cell becomes old, damaged or infected by a virus caspase enzymes will be activated.
This will cause the cell's cytoskeleton to breakdown.
This will cause the DNA to break up into small fragments.
These fragments will then be put into membrane bound vesicles.
The immune system will come to assist in digesting these vesicles,so they do not cause any toxic molecules to enter the surrounding tissue.
Apoptosis plays an important role during embryonic development.
We start off with solid paddles on our hands and feet.
The tissue between our fingers and toes will  go through apoptosis to create our digits.
Many redundant neurons in the developing brain will go through apoptosis.
If apoptosis does not occur properly this can lead to serious diseases.
Cancer occurs because there is no apoptosis occuring.
Proteins such as p53 are known as guardian proteins and can sense serious damage to DNA.
This will cause apoptosis to occur.
However many tumours have mutations meaning this pathway will not work .
This means the damaged cells will not go through apoptosis and will divide uncontrollably.
If there is too much apoptosis this can cause tissue loss in neurodegenerative diseases such as Alzheimers. 
Our knowledge of apoptosis has led to new treatments for oncology.
Scientists are working very hard to create drugs that will target cancerous cells and cause them to undergo apoptosis.
Overall this shows some individual cells must go through apoptosis to maintain life within the organism .
